From the lab activities, I had the ability to learn about the differences that exist between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). Before completing this lab, I viewed cloud computing mainly as a way of accessing storage online and application software. Through research and comparative analysis in this activity, I learned that cloud platforms provide many other services apart from just storage. Such services include virtual machines, networking, identity management, database, analytics, artificial intelligence, and much more.

One of the most important things that I learned was the fact that although AWS, Azure, and GCP provide similar services, there are differences in terms of advantages that each platform provides. AWS is a great cloud platform that offers a variety of services. Azure has some advantages for companies that use products such as Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Microsoft Entra ID. My interest in GCP was drawn by its artificial intelligence services, machine learning services, data analytics, and Kubernetes.

From the client recommendation assignment, I also learned that choosing a cloud service should be done according to the specific needs of the organization and not merely by picking the most popular one. Different cloud service providers and services could be suitable for different organizations, such as startups, universities, AI firms, and international e-commerce businesses.

One more thing that I gained from the KillerCoda assignment is the opportunity to use Linux commands to analyze a Linux environment and see what operating system it uses, as well as the CPU, RAM, and disk space available. I found out that Amazon EC2, Azure Virtual Machines, or Google Compute Engine can be used to deploy a similar Linux server.

Taking all things into consideration, the lab helped me enhance my understanding of multi-cloud computing and how different cloud services can be selected based on technical requirements, scalability, current infrastructure, and organizational goals. Moreover, it became easier for me to evaluate different cloud solutions and their potential usage.
